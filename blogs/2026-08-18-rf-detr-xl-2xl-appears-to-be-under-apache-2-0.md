Hey, thanks for the comment! The licensing is correct here – for instance segmentation, XL and 2XL are on Apache 2.0 as they are based off of the dinov2 small backbone. For object detection, XL and 2XL are on PML-1.0 as they are based off the dinov2 base backbone.
