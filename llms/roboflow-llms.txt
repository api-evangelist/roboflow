# Roboflow

> Roboflow is the computer vision platform used by over 1 million engineers to annotate images, train models, and deploy them to production — from the cloud to the edge.

The links below are a curated map of the most useful pages for understanding what Roboflow offers. For the full catalog of individual model pages, see the Roboflow Playground model directory (linked under Resources); for every page on the site, see the sitemap at https://roboflow.com/sitemap.xml.

## Product

- [Annotate](https://roboflow.com/annotate): Label images faster with AI-assisted annotation tools.
- [Train](https://roboflow.com/train): Train custom computer vision models on your own datasets.
- [Deploy](https://roboflow.com/deploy): Run production vision models at scale, in the cloud or on-device.
- [Workflows](https://roboflow.com/workflows/build): Open-source low-code tool to build multi-step vision pipelines.
- [Auto Label](https://roboflow.com/auto-label): Automatically label datasets using foundation models.
- [Universe](https://roboflow.com/universe): The largest community library of open vision datasets and models.
- [Managed Compute](https://roboflow.com/managed-compute): Hosted, autoscaling inference infrastructure.
- [Edge Vision Pro](https://roboflow.com/roboflow-edge-vision-pro): On-device vision AI with or without internet.
- [OCR](https://roboflow.com/ocr): Easy online optical character recognition.
